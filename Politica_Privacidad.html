Privacy Policy for 3DM QR-Scan

Effective Date: September 10, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to the 3DM QR-Scan mobile application (the "App"), developed for use with 3DM interactive topographic map products. We value your privacy and are committed to protecting any personal information that may be accessed through the App.

2. Information Collection and Use

  • Camera Access: The App requires access to your device’s camera strictly for the purpose of scanning QR codes located on 3DM topographic map pieces. The camera stream is processed locally on your device using Google ML Kit barcode scanning technology.
  • Data Storage: The App does not capture, store, transmit, or process any photos, video recordings, or personal data from your camera or device.
  • Personal Data: We do not collect, request, or store any personally identifiable information (such as names, email addresses, phone numbers, or locations).

3. Third-Party Services

The App utilizes Google ML Kit for local QR code detection. For more details on Google’s data privacy practices, please refer to the Google Privacy Policy.

4. External Links

When a QR code is scanned, the App may redirect you to external educational resources or video content hosted on third-party platforms. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of these external sites.

5. Children's Privacy

Our App does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13, as no personal data is collected from any user.
